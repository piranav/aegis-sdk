# Refund policy

- Unopened items: full refund within 30 days of delivery.
- Opened items: refund minus a 15% restocking fee within 14 days, if all parts are included.
- Defective items: full refund or replacement within 12 months, no restocking fee.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

Always quote the rule that applies and the deadline from the delivery date.
